CHIVAVA BUNDLED CONTENT

Prose excerpts are from public-domain books/translations in the USA.
Authors, works, edition URLs, and identified translators are in corpus.json.
Check copyright law in your country, especially for Hemingway (1926).
Typography, ligatures, and whitespace are normalized for typing practice.
Historical wording and viewpoints belong to their authors.

Code excerpts in code.json are from:
Django (BSD-3-Clause), Requests (Apache-2.0), VS Code (MIT),
TypeScript (Apache-2.0), Tokio (MIT), ripgrep (MIT),
Go standard library (BSD-3-Clause), Kubernetes (Apache-2.0),
Godot demos (MIT), and Pixelorama (MIT).
Comments and Python docstrings are removed from modified excerpts;
modifications are identified in code.json.
Pinned revisions, source ranges, and hashes are in code-provenance.json.
Upstream licenses, notices, and source-file copyright headers are retained
in licenses/code-projects.txt and printed by chivava licenses.
